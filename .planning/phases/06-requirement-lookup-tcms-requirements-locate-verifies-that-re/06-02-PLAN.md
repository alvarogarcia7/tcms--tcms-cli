Output contract (implement exactly; tests in plan 06-03 assert these strings):
- Found, Jira: `PAY-2: found in jira (jira) "Pay by card" [In progress] <url>` where the first `jira` is the family (`source`), the parenthesis is the system name (`Resolved.system`), the title is quoted (empty title prints `""`), the `[status]` group is omitted when the status is absent, `<url>` is `Resolved.url`.
- Found, StrictDoc: `HLR-12: found in strictdoc (strictdoc) at level HLR "Update profile metadata over SMS" [approved] <url>`; SYS-4 likewise with `at level system` (D-05).
- Not found: `PAY-99: not found in jira (jira)` and `HLR-99: not found in strictdoc (strictdoc)`.
- text format: each line prefixed `✓ ` (found) or `✗ ` (not found), then a final line `N of M requirement IDs found`. ANSI colour (green for found, red for not found) only when stdout is a terminal (`std::io::IsTerminal`) and the env var `NO_COLOR` is unset.
- plaintext format: only the bare lines, no marker, no colour, no summary line.
- json format: `serde_json::to_string_pretty` of an array of objects with keys `id`, `found` (bool), `source` ("jira" or "strictdoc"), `system`, `level` (string or null; Jira always null), `title` (string or null), `status` (string or null), `url`; followed by a newline.
- Order: command-line order; a repeated ID is reported once, at its first position.
- Exit 1: after printing, stderr line `not found: N of M requirement IDs` (all formats).
- Exit 2: stderr `error: {e:#}`; stdout empty (every check and lookup completes before anything is printed).
