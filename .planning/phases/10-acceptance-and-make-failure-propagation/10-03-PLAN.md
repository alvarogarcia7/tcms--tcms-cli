<objective>
Make `make acceptance-test` exit non-zero on any stage failure, and get stages 8 and 9 to pass or
record them as known failures with a backlog reference. Covers TCMS-45 acceptance criteria 2 and 3.

Current state on `dev`: the Makefile already captures the suite exit status through a pipe and
returns 1 on a non-zero status. The gaps are inside the suite: stages 8, 9 and 10 set `overall_success`
only through their own script exit codes, a zero-input stage passes silently (fixed in 10-01), and
nothing records a known failure. Stage 8 and Stage 9 have never been observed passing in this checkout;
their failure causes are unknown until Task 1 runs them.

Decision (D-45-3): the known-failure list is a file. A listed stage failure is shown as KNOWN FAILURE and
does not fail the suite. An unlisted failure fails the suite. A listed id must exist as a backlog task.
Recommendation for the user: fix stages 8 and 9 in this plan wherever the cause is local to tcms-cli,
and list only what cannot be fixed here.

Purpose: a green `make acceptance-test` must mean every stage passed or a named, tracked failure is
known.
Output: exit contract in the suite, known-failure list, fixes for stages 8 and 9 (or listed entries).
</objective>
