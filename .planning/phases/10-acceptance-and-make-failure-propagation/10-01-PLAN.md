<objective>
Make Stage 1 of the acceptance suite find every test case file regardless of extension, and make an
empty discovery a failure. Covers TCMS-45 acceptance criterion 1.

Decision (D-45-1): match both `*.yml` and `*.yaml`. Renaming 89 files is higher risk (git churn,
references in docs and `validate-testcases` targets) and gains nothing. One shared function keeps
Stage 1 and Stage 2 in step.

Purpose: a `.yaml` test case added later must not be silently skipped, and a run with zero inputs
must not print SUCCESS.
Output: discovery library, updated Stage 1 and Stage 2, count-guard test.
</objective>
